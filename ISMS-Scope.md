# MediConnect Health
## Information Security Management System (ISMS) Scope

**Document Owner:** Information Security  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Classification:** Internal

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the boundaries and applicability of the MediConnect Health Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The ISMS is designed to support the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information and systems used to operate MediConnect Health's Patient Portal and supporting services.

## 2. Organization Overview

MediConnect Health is a fictional healthcare technology company with approximately 150 employees. The organization operates a cloud-hosted Patient Portal that enables patients to access medical information, schedule appointments, communicate with healthcare providers, upload medical documents, and update personal information.

MediConnect Health processes and stores sensitive information including Protected Health Information (PHI), Personally Identifiable Information (PII), employee information, authentication data, and confidential business information.

## 3. ISMS Scope

### 3.1 Business Processes

- **Patient Portal Operations:** Operation and maintenance of the Patient Portal used by patients to access healthcare information and services.
